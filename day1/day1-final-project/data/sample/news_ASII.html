Company news: PT Astra International Tbk (ASII)

Analysts expect Astra to raise capital spending as EV plans take shape

30 October 2026

Analysts at three Jakarta brokerages raised their estimates of Astra International's capital expenditure for 2026 and 2027 after the group confirmed its electric vehicle battery plant this week.

Most now expect group capital spending to rise by 15 to 25 percent next year. Several analysts said the group will likely use a mix of internal cash and bank borrowing to fund the construction phase before longer-term project financing is in place.

Market roundup: Jakarta index edges higher as banks and miners lead

29 October 2026

The Jakarta Composite Index closed slightly higher on Thursday, led by large banks and coal miners. Foreign investors were net buyers for a third day.

Among consumer and automotive names, Astra International gave back part of Wednesday's gain. Telecommunications stocks were mixed, and property developers fell after a weak sales update.

Astra confirms investment in electric vehicle battery plant in West Java

28 October 2026

PT Astra International Tbk confirmed on Wednesday that it will build an electric vehicle battery plant in West Java with a South Korean partner. The company said construction starts in November 2026.

Astra did not give the total cost of the project. A company spokesperson said the first phase will be funded from internal cash and existing bank facilities, and that the group is in talks with lenders about project financing for the later phases. Astra shares rose 8 percent on the day.

Astra's car sales slip in September as buyers wait for new models

14 October 2026

Car sales of the Astra group fell 6 percent in September from a year earlier, as buyers held back ahead of several new model launches planned for the fourth quarter.

Astra's share of the domestic car market stayed above 50 percent. Motorcycle sales were roughly flat.

Astra's new logistics arm signs first large contracts

2 October 2026

The logistics subsidiary that Astra International set up in August has signed its first contracts with two consumer goods companies, the group said.

The unit employs about 2,000 people and plans to expand its warehouse network in Java next year.

Weaker rupiah squeezes importers' margins in the third quarter

18 September 2026

Companies that pay for components in US dollars and Japanese yen faced higher costs in the third quarter as the rupiah weakened. Automotive groups, including Astra, have increased their use of currency hedging this year, according to people familiar with the matter.
